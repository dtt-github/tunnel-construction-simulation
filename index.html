Tunnel Construction Simulation

An open simulator for mechanised tunnelling: published analytical models in the browser, GPU ground physics on the server.

Recorded GPU runs

Material-point simulations recorded on the GPU server and replayed in the browser: a tunnel face collapsing as its support retracts, and an EPB chamber with a rotating cutterhead and screw conveyor. Includes live pressure and torque charts.

Hard-rock TBM demo

A single-shield machine driven by the CSM disc-cutter model: thrust, penetration, torque, the boring and ring-build cycle, and alarms.
Source and model references: github.com/dtt-github/tunnel-construction-simulation
