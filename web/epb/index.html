Model limits

  • Volume loss is a scenario input; it does not yet respond to face pressure or grouting.
  • Building categories use greenfield horizontal strain only (no deflection ratio, no building stiffness).
  • Advance per day counts the push and ring-build cycle only; downtime (logistics, maintenance, tool changes) is not modelled yet.
  • Chamber volume, screw efficiency, friction coefficients and machine weights are illustrative assumptions.
Models: wedge-silo face support and DAUB window, chamber volume balance with foam, simplified screw (Merritt & Mair), thrust and torque components (Shi et al. 2011), Gaussian settlement (Peck; O'Reilly & New; Mair & Taylor). Machine class from published references.
