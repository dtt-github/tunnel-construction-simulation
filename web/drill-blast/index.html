Model limits

  • The T10.5 profile is drawn as vertical walls with a semicircular roof of the same theoretical area (74.6 m²); the real profile is flatter.
  • The "Norwegian practice" pattern uses a contour spacing of 0.47 m and 0.75 of the Olofsson burden, fitted to published hole counts; it is not a design rule. Charges per hole follow the textbook rules.
  • Activities run one after another; on site some overlap (for example sprayed concrete during a standstill), so advance can reach 75 m/week. Charging time, hole positioning and the reinforced ribs time are assumed.
  • Vibration uses one published site fit (Stockholm Northern Link) and the building factors of one published Swedish risk analysis; the house and its distance are illustrative. Geology is illustrative.
Models: Holmberg cut and pull, Olofsson burden and charges (Yilmaz 2023), smooth contour (Dyno Nobel), Norwegian support classes (NFF; NGI Q-system), cycle rates (NFF Publications 14, 26, 28), scaled-distance vibration (Bahrekazemi et al. 2011) and SS 460 48 66 guide values.
