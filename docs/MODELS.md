# Model catalogue

Implemented models. Each source file states its references, equations and units; this page is the index.

| Model | Python | JS | References | Validated against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CSM disc cutter: forces, penetration from thrust, ROP, torque (T ≈ 0.3·D·N·Fr), power, torque limit | `models/py/tunnelsim_models/csm.py` | `models/js/csm.js` | Rostami & Ozdemir (1993), Rostami (1997) | Hand calculation for a 19″ disc (p = 5 mm, UCS 150, BTS 10 → Ft ≈ 201 kN, RC ≈ 0.10). | Gaussian settlement trough (symmetric or asymmetric), horizontal displacement, longitudinal profile built from face / shield / tail-void / behind-the-tail volume-loss components, excavation-method presets (VL, K), twin-tunnel interaction, Burland categories | `settlement.py` | `settlement.js` | Peck (1969); O'Reilly & New (1982); Attewell & Woodman (1982); Mair & Taylor (1997); Addenbrooke & Potts (2001, doi:10.1061/(ASCE)1532-3641(2001)1:2(249)); Burland (1995) | Hand calculation (D 6 m, z0 20 m, VL 1 % → Smax 11.3 mm); numerical integration equals Vs, also for asymmetric troughs; one component at the face gives half the settlement at the face; a tail-only component gives Φ(−L/i); the closed-face preset leaves 0.1–0.2 of the final trough at the face; twin defaults equal superposition. | Face support: wedge-silo limit equilibrium (Janssen silo, layered above/below the water table, hydrostatic water, no seepage yet), Vermeer cohesionless limit, DAUB operating window p_min/p_max (partial factors as parameters, defaults 1.5 / 1.05 / 0.9 not yet checked against the document) | `face_support.py` | `face_support.js` | Horn (1961); Janssen (1895); Anagnostou & Kovári (1994, doi:10.1016/0886-7798(94)90028-0); Vermeer, Ruse & Marcher (2002); DAUB/ITA-AITES (2016) | Silo limits (deep → (rγ − c)/(K tan φ), φ = 0 → overburden); dry sand within 1.0–1.3× Vermeer for φ 25–40°; nearly cover-independent; stiff undrained clay self-supporting; window closes at very shallow cover under water.
