## Before the next publish

- [~] Ring-build time and segment thickness for a 10 m double shield from a primary source. Bracketed (6 Nov 2026): ring build 15–67 min on 5–6.5 m machines; segments 450 mm on the 10.71 m Brenner machines.
- [~] Main thrust, auxiliary thrust and gripper force for a 10 m machine. Found for the 10.71 m Brenner machines: 95 MN nominal and 210 MN exceptional main thrust, up to 200 MN auxiliary thrust in single-shield mode. Gripper force for 10 m still not found.
- [~] Shield jamming check in squeezing ground (roadmap model 8): screening estimate done. At 300 m cover the fault zone needs about 148 MN sliding and 229 MN to restart, against up to 200 MN from the auxiliary cylinders in single-shield mode (Brenner 10.71 m), so the demo reports a jamming risk after any standstill. The alarm does not stop the drive. Creep and standstill time are still open. The 6.85 m Brenner exploratory machine was stuck for 32 and 46 days in fault zones.
